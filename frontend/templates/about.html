About the Project

The Landslide Risk Mapper is a full-stack geospatial intelligence platform developed for regional landslide susceptibility screening. It combines GIS-based environmental analysis with machine learning to visualize and assess areas that may have elevated landslide susceptibility.

Problem Statement

Landslides are influenced by multiple environmental conditions including terrain slope, elevation, rainfall, topography, and local geographic characteristics.

Mountainous regions can therefore contain areas with substantially different levels of susceptibility even within relatively short distances.

The problem is to provide an accessible, transparent, and interactive GIS platform that combines geospatial information with machine-learning-based susceptibility assessment to support regional screening and decision-making.

Solution Overview

The Landslide Risk Mapper provides an interactive web-based GIS dashboard where users can search for locations, inspect terrain and environmental information, visualize geographic layers, and obtain a Random Forest-based landslide susceptibility score.

🗺️ Interactive GIS Mapping

Interactive Leaflet mapping with OpenStreetMap, OpenTopoMap and satellite basemaps, clickable locations, coordinate analysis, and independent layer controls.

⛰️ Terrain & Slope Analysis

Retrieves elevation information and calculates terrain slope characteristics to identify locations with steep or rugged terrain.

🌧️ Rainfall Monitoring

Displays recent precipitation information, including antecedent rainfall and short-term precipitation patterns that provide environmental context.

🤖 Random Forest Risk Assessment

A Random Forest machine-learning classifier estimates landslide susceptibility using slope angle and elevation as its trained predictive features.

📍 Historical Landslide Evidence

Historical landslide information from the NASA Global Landslide Catalog provides additional regional context.

📊 Visual Analytics

Interactive charts display environmental indicators, precipitation patterns, and regional risk distributions.

Machine Learning Model

🌲 Random Forest Landslide Susceptibility Model

The machine-learning component uses a Random Forest classifier trained on a public geo-environmental landslide dataset.

The model predicts the probability that a location belongs to the landslide-susceptible class. This probability is converted into the dashboard's 0–100 ML risk score.

The trained model currently uses slope angle and elevation as its predictive features.

Live rainfall and other GIS information remain available on the dashboard as supporting environmental indicators but are not direct inputs to this trained Random Forest model.

Accuracy 75.99%
Precision 74.25%
Recall 83.23%
F1 Score 78.48%
ROC-AUC 83.05%

Training Dataset: A Geo-Environmental Feature Dataset for Landslide Susceptibility Modelling in Himachal Pradesh, India.

Dataset Size: 12,409 samples.

Training Samples: 9,927.

Testing Samples: 2,482.

Predictive Features: Slope Angle and Elevation.

Target: Binary landslide susceptibility classification.

Technology Stack

Backend: Python, Flask, Rasterio, GeoPandas, NumPy, Pandas, Shapely and SciPy.
Machine Learning: Scikit-learn Random Forest, train/test evaluation, probability-based susceptibility scoring.
Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript, Leaflet.js and Chart.js.
Geospatial Data: SRTM / ALOS / Copernicus DEM, Open-Meteo precipitation services, NASA Global Landslide Catalog, OpenStreetMap and OpenTopoMap.
ML Dataset: Mendeley Data — Geo-Environmental Feature Dataset for Landslide Susceptibility Modelling.

Responsible Use

Screening-Level Assessment: The Landslide Risk Mapper is designed for regional susceptibility screening, academic demonstration, visualization, and decision support.

The ML score should not be interpreted as a precise prediction of when or where a landslide will occur. The system does not replace detailed geotechnical investigations, field surveys, local monitoring, or official warnings issued by relevant authorities.
